Privacy policy

Family Dashboard · last updated 21 October 2026

What it accesses

With your permission, Family Dashboard gets read-only access to your Google Calendar. It reads events from the calendars the household has chosen. It cannot create, change or delete anything in your calendar.

How the data is used

Event titles, times and calendar names are shown on the household's own display at home. They are not used for anything else.

Where the data is kept

Everything stays on the household's own home server. Events are kept in memory for a few minutes, to avoid asking Google too often. The sign-in token is stored encrypted on that server. Nothing is sent to anyone else, sold, or used for advertising.

Family Dashboard's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Who can see it

The members of the household, on their display and their devices at home.

Removing access

You can remove Family Dashboard's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The household can also delete the stored token on its server.

Contact

Through the GitHub account that publishes this page.
