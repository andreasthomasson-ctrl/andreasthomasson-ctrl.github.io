Privacy policy

Family Dashboard · last updated 1 November 2026

What it accesses

With your permission, Family Dashboard gets two kinds of access to Google Calendar:

  • Read-only access to your calendars. It reads events from the calendars the household has chosen, and cannot create, change or delete anything in them.
  • Access to one calendar it creates itself. It adds, changes and removes events only in that calendar, and only when someone in the household asks it to. It cannot change any other calendar.

How the data is used

Event titles, times, places and calendar names are shown on the household's own display at home and in the household's own app on their phones. They are not used for anything else.

Where the data is kept

Everything stays on the household's own home server. Events are kept in memory for a few minutes, to avoid asking Google too often. The server also keeps the identifiers of the calendar it created and of the events in it, to keep track of them. The sign-in token is stored encrypted on that server. Data from Google is not sent to anyone else, sold, or used for advertising.

Family Dashboard's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Who can see it

The members of the household, on their display and their devices at home.

Removing access

You can remove Family Dashboard's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The household can also delete the stored token on its server. The calendar Family Dashboard created stays in your Google account until you delete it there.
